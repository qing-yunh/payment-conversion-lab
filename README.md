# Payment Conversion Lab

An interactive product case study by **Jingyun He**: how to identify a payment conversion problem, evaluate options, plan an experiment and make an evidence-based decision.

**All data and experiment results are synthetic.** This demonstrates a decision-making process, not proven business growth or a production payment system.

## Open it

- Live demo: https://qing-yunh.github.io/payment-conversion-lab/
- Start with the **5-minute tour**: seven steps from the first signal on the payment funnel to a recommended decision.
- Everything runs in your browser. The sample data is generated on your device and kept in this browser's storage; nothing is sent to a server.
- Tested in recent versions of Chrome and Edge.

## What it shows

- Payment funnel monitoring and a statistically corrected search for the segment where conversion dropped.
- Possible causes kept as hypotheses, options sized from the data, and an experiment plan sealed before any result.
- Simulated A/B results read with checks first: guardrails, missing data and incomplete exports change the conclusion.
- A recommended decision with its boundary: **Planning Ready means preparing an independent real experiment, never approving a rollout.**
